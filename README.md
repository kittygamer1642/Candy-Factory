<h1>Candy Factory</h1>
<h2>FNAF inspired survival horror game</h2>
<h2>Made in a week for a Halloween event in my computer science club</h2>
Your goal is to sort the candy while keeping enemies out of your office using the doors.
You can use your cameras as well as your flashlight to track enemy movements.
Be carful not to keep doors closed for too long as well as using the cams too much. You could run out of power, leaving you defensless!
